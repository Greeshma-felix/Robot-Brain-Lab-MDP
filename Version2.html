Why transparency matters

Robots, medical planners and automated trading policies must justify decisions after the fact. A log of options, scores, risks and outcomes is an audit trail: it lets a person check why an action was chosen, find a badly set reward, and trust or correct the system. This is the idea behind explainable reinforcement learning.
